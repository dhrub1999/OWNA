Nothing clever is happening to your page.

No audience to grow, no algorithm, no analytics you didn't ask for. It is a page, at a URL, that you control.

Free while OWNA is this young. A paid tier will only arrive for things that don't exist yet — more than one page, a domain of your own — and nothing you build today gets taken away.

Your draft is private until you say otherwise

A page is invisible until you publish it, and unpublishing takes it straight back down. As often as you like.

Only you can write to it

Every change is made as you, enforced by the database itself. There is no admin key in the product that could edit your page on your behalf.

Links and embeds are checked, not trusted

A pasted URL is matched against a fixed list of providers before anything renders, so an embed is never arbitrary code on your page.

The handle stays the same

Publishing swaps the page behind owna.online/yourname in about a second — no rebuild, no new URL. A printed card or a bio link never goes stale.

Free, for now.

Every block, every theme, your own handle, publish and unpublish as often as you want. No card, no trial clock.
